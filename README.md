About Me

I work at the intersection of **European power markets**, **quantitative modeling**, and **AI-driven risk intelligence**.  
My focus is building systems that extract actionable insights from price, load, RES, and imbalance fundamentals.

I am the creator of the **RIM Engine DE-LU**, a 4-factor risk intelligence model that transforms raw market data into:

- a **0–100 interpretable risk score**,  
- **market regime classification**,  
- **AI-generated analyst, forecaster, and evaluator outputs**.

These tools are designed for **short-term trading**, **volatility forecasting**, and **grid-driven risk detection**.
